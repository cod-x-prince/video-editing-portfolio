# Music Placeholders

Drop your songs in this folder using these names:

- `track-1.mp3`
- `track-2.mp3`
- `track-3.mp3`

The playlist metadata lives in `data/tracks.ts`. Once the files exist, the
music player will try to start 3 seconds after the hero appears and fade in over
roughly 2.6 seconds. If the browser blocks autoplay, the player will show a
tap-to-play state.
